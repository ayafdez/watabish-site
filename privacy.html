privacy policy

Effective October 22, 2026

the short version

  • We only collect what we need to paint your pet and run the game.
  • We never sell your information, show you ads, or track you around the internet.
  • The game works without an account. Signing in is optional and only saves your progress.
  • You can ask us to see, fix or delete your information at any time.

1. who we are

watabish is a small, mostly-for-fun project run by Aya and Grace in Ontario, Canada, inspired by a cat named Chikadee Watabish. "We," "us" and "our" mean watabish. Questions about privacy go to aya@likhangmaya.com. Aya and Grace are responsible for privacy at watabish.

2. what we collect

When you book a portrait (through our booking page, powered by Cal.com):

  • your name and email address
  • your pet's name and species, a photo of your pet, and anything you tell us about them
  • your booking date and where works for the hand-off

When you pay, Stripe processes your card. We see that you paid and the last four digits of your card. We never see or store your full card number.

When you play the game without an account, your progress is saved only in your own browser on your own device. We don't receive it.

When you sign in to the game (optional):

  • your email address, or your name and email from Google if you sign in with Google
  • a random account ID
  • your game progress (hearts, tokens, items, collected wisdom) and when it was last saved

When you message us, we receive whatever you send.

What we don't do: no advertising, no analytics or tracking cookies, no selling or renting your information. Our hosting provider (GitHub) may keep basic technical logs, like IP addresses, to keep the site running and secure.

3. why we use it

  • to paint your portrait, contact you about your order and arrange the hand-off
  • to take payment and keep the records the law requires for a small business
  • to save and sync your game progress if you sign in
  • to answer your messages and keep the site and game secure

We only send you emails about your order or your account. If we ever start a newsletter, it'll be opt-in.

4. your pet's photo and portrait

We use your pet's photo only to paint the portrait. We'll only post your pet's portrait (in our portfolio or on social media) if you say yes. You can change your mind at any time and we'll take it down.

6. how long we keep it

  • Booking and payment records: as long as the law requires for business records (in Canada, generally six years), then deleted.
  • Your pet's photo: deleted within 90 days after the hand-off, unless you ask us to keep it.
  • Game accounts: until you delete your account, or after two years without signing in.

7. your choices

You can ask us to show you what we have about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. Email aya@likhangmaya.com and we'll respond within 30 days. Deleting your game account erases your saved progress for good. We may need to keep some booking records if the law requires it.

8. kids

Anyone can play the game without an account. Game accounts are for people 13 and older. Portrait bookings are for adults, or for younger people with a parent or guardian's permission. If you think a child under 13 created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

9. keeping it safe

We use reputable services and reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No system is perfect, though. If a breach puts you at real risk of significant harm, we'll tell you and report it as Canadian law requires.

10. changes and complaints

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, for big changes, let account holders know. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please talk to us first. You can also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
