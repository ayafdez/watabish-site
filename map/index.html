how it works

the map

Neighbours post the cats they meet. Each cat gets one profile that collects sightings, photos and comments, so everyone knows who's who.

Spotted is a cat doing cat things. Needs help is a cat that's cold, hungry or hurt. Maybe lost is a cat or dog that looks like someone's missing pet. When it's found, whoever posted it can mark it back home and it leaves the map.

A Guardian is the neighbour who looks after a cat: feeds it, keeps an eye out, organizes help. Anyone with an account can step up for a cat that doesn't have one.

about money

Never send money, gift cards, crypto or e-transfers to people you meet here. Watabish doesn't collect donations for anyone, and nobody on the map works for us. We can't check who people are.

Want to help with costs? Give to a registered rescue or shelter directly, or drop off food and supplies yourself.

Lost pet? Someone who really found it won't need a \u201cshipping fee\u201d, deposit or verification code first. Meet in a public place, and bring a friend.

keeping cats (and you) safe

Locations on the map are moved a little on purpose. Cats who need help or might be lost only show as an area. When you post a photo, the location info hidden inside it is removed before it's uploaded.

If a cat is badly hurt or in danger, contact your local animal services or a rescue right away. Don't try to catch a scared or feral cat yourself; rescues have traps and know-how.

Think a pet is lost? Look for a collar or tag, and ask a vet or shelter to scan it for a microchip. It's free.

Dogs are only on the map as maybe lost. A loose dog that seems aggressive or injured is a job for animal control, not neighbours.
