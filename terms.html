2. pet portraits

What you get. A hand-painted 5×7 inch acrylic portrait of your pet on canvas. The bish-signed edition also includes a Watabish paw print signature. Because every portrait is painted by hand, colours, brushstrokes and details will differ from photos and from other portraits.

Price and payment. Prices are in Canadian dollars as shown on the site when you book, and are paid in full at booking by card through Stripe. Tips are optional and non-refundable.

Your photo. Please send a clear, well-lit photo of your pet's face. By sending it, you confirm you took it or have permission to share it. If the photo isn't usable, we'll ask for another one.

Timing. Turnaround is about one week after we have a usable photo. It's an estimate, not a promise. If anything changes, we'll let you know.

Hand-off. There's no shipping. We meet in person at an agreed public spot (usually a very nice tree) to hand over your portrait. If you miss the hand-off, we'll reschedule. If we can't arrange a hand-off within 60 days of the portrait being ready, we'll treat the order as complete.

Cancellations and refunds. Cancel before we start painting and you'll get a full refund. Once painting has started, we can't offer a refund. If something's off, tell us at the hand-off and we'll fix anything reasonable. If we can't complete your portrait for any reason, you'll get a full refund.

Ownership and sharing. You own the physical portrait. We keep the copyright in the artwork, which means you can hang it, gift it and post photos of it, but not sell prints or merchandise of it. We'll only post your pet's portrait publicly if you say yes.
