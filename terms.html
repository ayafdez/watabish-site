2. pet portraits

What you get. A hand-painted 8×10 inch acrylic portrait of your pet on canvas. The bish-signed edition also includes a Watabish paw print signature. A portrait can include a second pet for an extra $30 (two pets maximum). Because every portrait is painted by hand, colours, brushstrokes and details will differ from photos and from other portraits.

Price and payment. Prices are in Canadian dollars as shown on the site when you book, and are paid in full at checkout on our website, processed securely by Stripe. Your painting week is confirmed once payment goes through. Tips are optional and non-refundable.

Your photo. Please send a clear, well-lit photo of your pet's face. By sending it, you confirm you took it or have permission to share it. If the photo isn't usable, we'll ask for another one.

Timing. When you order, you choose the week we start painting, at least one week away. We paint up to four portraits a week, and your portrait is usually ready to ship (or pick up) about a week after your painting week starts. Please send a usable photo within 48 hours of ordering. If the photo arrives late or anything else changes, we'll let you know and agree on a new week.

Free pick-up in Sault Ste. Marie. Available at checkout for Sault Ste. Marie addresses. We'll message you when it's ready. We meet in person at an agreed public spot (usually a very nice tree) to hand over your portrait. If you miss the hand-off, we'll reschedule. If we can't arrange a hand-off within 60 days of the portrait being ready, we'll treat the order as complete.

Shipping. Shipping is calculated from your address at checkout. Within Canada it starts at $28, depending on your province, and each extra portrait in the same parcel adds a few dollars. Portraits ship by tracked, insured parcel, and you'll get the tracking number by email. Delivery usually takes 2 to 7 business days, longer for remote areas. Please make sure your address is right: if a parcel comes back because of a wrong address, re-shipping costs extra. Once the tracking shows it delivered, it's in your hands.

Shipping to the United States. US shipping is $39, plus $8 for each extra portrait in the same parcel. The price includes tracked, insured shipping and any US customs duties and clearance fees, which we prepay. Delivery usually takes 5 to 10 business days and can be delayed by customs.

Shipping to other countries. We also ship to a small list of other countries, shown at checkout. The shipping price is shown before you pay. Import duties, taxes or fees charged by the destination country are not included and are paid by the recipient.

Multiple portraits. You can add several portraits to one order. Portraits going to the same address ship together, and shipping is calculated for the whole parcel at checkout.

Gift cards. Gift cards are emailed to the recipient as a code they can use toward any portrait at checkout. They don't expire and can't be exchanged for cash.

Damaged in shipping. We pack every portrait carefully, but if yours arrives damaged, send us a photo of the portrait and the box within 48 hours of delivery. We'll repaint it and ship it again for free, or give you a full refund, whichever you prefer. If a parcel is lost, we'll work it out with the carrier and repaint or refund you.

Cancellations and refunds. You can cancel for a full refund up to 7 days before your painting week starts. Within 7 days of that, we can't offer a refund, because your portrait is about to be painted. If something's off, tell us at the hand-off (or within 48 hours of delivery) and we'll fix anything reasonable. If we can't complete your portrait for any reason, you'll get a full refund.

Ownership and sharing. You own the physical portrait. We keep the copyright in the artwork, which means you can hang it, gift it and post photos of it, but not sell prints or merchandise of it. We'll only post your pet's portrait publicly if you say yes.
