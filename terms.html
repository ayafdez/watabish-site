2. pet portraits

What you get. A hand-painted 8×10 inch acrylic portrait of your pet on canvas. The bish-signed edition also includes a Watabish paw print signature. Because every portrait is painted by hand, colours, brushstrokes and details will differ from photos and from other portraits.

Price and payment. Prices are in Canadian dollars as shown on the site when you book, and are paid in full at booking through our Cal.com booking page, processed securely by Stripe. Your slot is confirmed once payment goes through. Bookings that are still unpaid after 48 hours are cancelled and the slot is released. Tips are optional and non-refundable.

Your photo. Please send a clear, well-lit photo of your pet's face. By sending it, you confirm you took it or have permission to share it. If the photo isn't usable, we'll ask for another one.

Timing. The date you book is the date your portrait ships (or, for local pick-up, your pick-up date), at least one week away so there's time to paint. Please send a usable photo within 48 hours of booking. If the photo arrives late or anything else changes, we'll let you know and agree on a new date.

Free pick-up in Sault Ste. Marie. Available on request: message us before booking and we'll arrange it. We meet in person at an agreed public spot (usually a very nice tree) to hand over your portrait. If you miss the hand-off, we'll reschedule. If we can't arrange a hand-off within 60 days of the portrait being ready, we'll treat the order as complete.

Shipping. We ship anywhere in Canada for a flat $27, which is included in the price of a shipped booking. Orders ship by tracked parcel on or soon after the date you booked, and you'll get the tracking number by text or email. Delivery usually takes 2 to 7 business days, longer for remote areas. Please make sure your address is right: if a parcel comes back because of a wrong address, re-shipping costs another $27. Once the tracking shows it delivered, it's in your hands.

Shipping to the United States. US shipping is available by special arrangement through a private booking link. The US price includes tracked shipping and any US customs duties and clearance fees, which we prepay. Delivery usually takes 5 to 10 business days and can be delayed by customs. We ship to the 48 mainland states; Alaska, Hawaii and US territories are quoted separately. The same damage and lost-parcel terms apply.

Multiple portraits. Several portraits going to the same address can ship together for one combined shipping price. Email us for a quote; we'll send a total and a secure payment link, and your order is confirmed once that's paid.

Damaged in shipping. We pack every portrait carefully, but if yours arrives damaged, send us a photo of the portrait and the box within 48 hours of delivery. We'll repaint it and ship it again for free, or give you a full refund, whichever you prefer. If a parcel is lost, we'll work it out with the carrier and repaint or refund you.

Cancellations and refunds. You can cancel for a full refund up to 7 days before your ship date (or pick-up date). Within 7 days of that date, we can't offer a refund, because your portrait is already being painted. If something's off, tell us at the hand-off (or within 48 hours of delivery) and we'll fix anything reasonable. If we can't complete your portrait for any reason, you'll get a full refund.

Ownership and sharing. You own the physical portrait. We keep the copyright in the artwork, which means you can hang it, gift it and post photos of it, but not sell prints or merchandise of it. We'll only post your pet's portrait publicly if you say yes.
