prices are in Canadian dollars (CAD). your bank converts them to US dollars when you pay, so the amount on your statement will be a little lower. some US cards add a small foreign-purchase fee.

no surprise fees at your door

US customs fees are already paid. since 2025, every parcel coming into the US goes through customs, even small ones. we prepay any duties and clearance fees when we ship, and they're included in the price above. you won't get a bill from the carrier. every parcel is also insured.

the parcel is declared honestly as an original hand-painted painting, made in Canada.

how it works

  1. pick your ship date and paychoose the day you'd like it shipped, at least a week out, and pay in full at checkout. your slot is confirmed once payment goes through.
  2. send your pet's photo within 48 hoursa clear, well-lit photo of their face. reply to your confirmation email (or dm @watabish_).
  3. i paint it, by handacrylic on an 8×10 inch stretched canvas. the bish supervises (judges).
  4. it ships, trackedwrapped in parchment, cardboard and bubble wrap. you get the tracking number by email on the day it ships.
  5. it arrivesusually 5 to 10 business days after shipping. customs can occasionally add a few days.

good to know

  • where we ship: all 48 mainland states. Alaska, Hawaii and US territories cost more to reach, so message us first and we'll quote you.
  • your address: please double-check it when you book, including the ZIP code. if a parcel comes back because of a wrong address, re-shipping costs extra.
  • arrived damaged? send a photo of the portrait and the box within 48 hours of delivery. we'll repaint it and ship it again for free, or refund you in full, your choice.
  • lost in transit? we'll work it out with the carrier and repaint or refund you.
  • cancelling: cancel before we start painting for a full refund.
